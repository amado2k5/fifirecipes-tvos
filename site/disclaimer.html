Food safety

Quantities, times and temperatures may be approximate. Cook food to safe internal temperatures and follow your local food-safety authority (for example Health Canada and the CFIA, FoodSafety.gov in the United States, or EFSA and your national authority in Europe).

Allergens

Allergen warnings are generated from ingredient names. They can be wrong or missing and cannot see cross-contact or brand formulations. “No allergen found” never means “safe for an allergy”. Read every product label and follow your doctor's advice.

Halal and other dietary labels

Halal, kosher, vegetarian, vegan and similar labels describe the listed ingredients as screened by a program. They are not religious rulings or certifications; the products, preparation and utensils you use also matter.

Health and nutrition

Nutrition figures, the diabetic estimate and health tags are rough approximations. Nothing in the app is medical, nutritional or dietary advice.

Cooking with Kids

Steps with a knife, heat, a blender or an oven are marked for a grown-up, but an adult should supervise every step.
