Support

Getting around

Use the Siri Remote touch surface or arrow buttons to move focus, the center button to select, and Menu to go back.

Changing language

Go to the Settings tab → Language. FiFi Recipes supports 26 languages including Arabic, Hebrew, Persian, Urdu, Kurdish, and Pashto (right-to-left).

Videos

Recipe videos open in the YouTube app on Apple TV. If it isn’t installed, install it free from the App Store.

Contact

Email support@fifi.cooking. You can also browse all recipes on the web at fifi.cooking.
