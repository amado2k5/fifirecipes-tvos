Privacy Policy

FiFi Recipes for Apple TV — effective September 2025.

What we collect

Nothing. The app collects no personal information, no analytics, and no usage data.

What stays on your device

Your chosen language and cooking progress are stored locally on your Apple TV (standard app preferences). Nothing is uploaded.

Network requests

The app downloads public recipe data and images from fifi.cooking (static files on GitHub Pages) and loads video links from YouTube. These requests include your IP address and standard HTTP headers, like any web request.

Third parties

Recipe videos open in the YouTube app when installed; YouTube’s own privacy policy applies there.

Children

Kids mode contains no accounts, no purchases, and no data collection.
